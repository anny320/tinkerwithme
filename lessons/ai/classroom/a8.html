AI Careers Deep Dive

Intermediate · about 60 minutes · ages 13–16 · classroom

{{parent}}, please read first: A discussion-and-research lesson. Any AI use is supervised and optional; it pairs well with local career context and guest input.

1. Session Overview

Students explore the breadth of AI careers — not just programmers, but ethicists, data scientists, designers, researchers, and domain experts in health, agriculture, art and law. The goal is to widen students' sense of possibility and connect AI to their own strengths and community.

2. Learning Objectives

  • Name several AI roles and what each does.
  • Connect AI to careers beyond "tech".
  • Identify needed skills, including non-coding ones.
  • Relate roles to their own interests.

3. Materials & Setup

  • Devices for research (supervised) or printed role cards.
  • A "careers map" worksheet.

4. Minute-by-Minute Schedule (planned session: {{duration}})

  • 0:00–0:10 — Hook: class brainstorms every job that touches AI.
  • 0:10–0:35 — Jigsaw: each group researches a role, then teaches the class.
  • 0:35–0:55 — Connect: students match their interests to a role.
  • 0:55–1:00 — Share one role each would try.

5. Activity Breakdown

Roles (jigsaw one per group)

Success criteria: a student can describe 3+ AI careers and link one to something they enjoy.

6. Assessment

  • Each group teaches its role in 60 seconds (peer-assessed for clarity).
  • Exit ticket: "one AI role I could see myself in, and why."

7. Extensions & Challenges

  • Design an AI job that doesn't exist yet; interview a family member about AI in their work.

8. Troubleshooting Guide

  • Feels abstract: anchor each role to a concrete example from earlier lessons.

9. Classroom Management Tips

{{parent}}, the jigsaw method keeps everyone active and covers all roles quickly. Emphasise that AI needs many kinds of thinkers — not only mathematicians. Where possible, invite a local professional to add real context.

10. Follow-up Activities

  • Students make a one-page "future me" poster for an AI-related role.
