AI Music & Sound

Beginner · about 60 minutes · ages 7–10 · classroom

{{parent}}, please read first: Use a family-friendly AI music tool on a facilitator device with class speakers. Preview audio before playing to the group.

1. Session Overview

The class composes music with AI, describing mood, tempo and instruments and hearing it come to life. Students build music vocabulary and see that the "describe it well" idea from art and writing applies to sound too.

2. Learning Objectives

  • Describe music by mood, tempo, instruments, style.
  • Iterate a prompt to change the feel.
  • Recognise how music sets a scene.
  • Discuss how AI learns from existing music.

3. Materials & Setup

  • Facilitator device + class speakers; AI music generator.
  • Whiteboard for mood/instrument words.

4. Minute-by-Minute Schedule (planned session: {{duration}})

  • 0:00–0:10 — Hook: play clips; class guesses the mood.
  • 0:10–0:35 — Create: groups suggest moods/instruments; facilitator generates.
  • 0:35–0:50 — Soundtrack a scene: "chase" vs "calm morning".
  • 0:50–1:00 — Share favourites.

5. Activity Breakdown

Music-prompt frame

"[Mood] music for [scene], [tempo], with [instruments], in a [style]."

  • "Happy, bouncy party music, fast, with ukulele and claps."
  • "Spooky, mysterious music for a haunted house, slow, with low piano and strings."
Success criteria: the class changes the mood word and hears the feeling change.

6. Assessment

  • Groups match three clips to three scenes and justify.
  • Discussion: "which instrument creates the mood?"

7. Extensions & Challenges

  • Make a class theme song; switch two contrasting tracks to tell a mini story.

8. Troubleshooting Guide

  • Doesn't match mood: lead with the mood word; name instruments.

9. Classroom Management Tips

{{parent}}, control volume and use a clear "listening now" signal. Collect group ideas on the board first, then generate a curated few — this keeps a lively activity orderly and ensures broad participation.

10. Follow-up Activities

  • Pair an AI track with an AI image from the Art Studio lesson for a "mood card" display.
