Mashup Madness

Intermediate · about 90 minutes · ages 10–13

{{parent}}, please read first: This lesson chains several parent-operated AI tools (text, image, music). You run the accounts and preview each output; {{child}} directs and assembles.

1. Session Overview

{{child}} becomes a creative director, combining several AI tools into one project — for example an illustrated, narrated mini-story with a soundtrack. The lesson is about workflow: using the right tool for each job and making the pieces fit together, which is exactly how professionals use AI.

2. Learning Objectives

  • Plan a multi-step creative project and pick the right AI tool for each step.
  • Pass ideas between tools (story → images → music).
  • Keep a consistent theme across different media.
  • Assemble the parts into one finished piece.

3. Materials & Setup

  • Computer/tablet with internet.
  • Parent-operated AI text, image, and music tools.
  • A slideshow or document app to assemble the final piece.

4. Flexible Schedule (planned session: {{duration}})

  • Plan the mashup (15 min): Choose a theme and the steps.
  • Make the parts (45 min): Story text, then matching images, then a soundtrack.
  • Assemble (25 min): Combine into a slideshow or "story card".
  • Present (5 min): Share the finished mashup.

5. Activity Breakdown

Example project: "A Day in the Life of My Invention"

  1. Text tool: "Write a 6-sentence story about a kid inventor and their robot pet, for a 10-year-old."
  2. Image tool: illustrate 3 scenes — keep the same style word each time (e.g. "cartoon, bright colours") so they match.
  3. Music tool: "Cheerful, adventurous background music, medium tempo, with ukulele."
  4. Assemble: put the images + text on slides; play the music behind them.
Success looks like: a single themed piece that combines at least three AI outputs, with {{child}} explaining which tool did which job.

6. Conversation Starters & Check-ins

  • "Which tool was best for which job? Why?"
  • "How did you keep everything looking/feeling like one project?"
  • "What was the hardest part to make fit together?"

7. Extensions & Challenges

  • Add a title screen and credits ("Directed by {{child}}").
  • Make a 3-slide advert for a made-up product.

8. Troubleshooting Guide

  • Images don't match: reuse the exact same style words and colours in every image prompt.
  • Too much to manage: keep it to 3 scenes; quality over quantity.

9. Parent Guidance Tips

{{parent}}, the skill here is orchestration. Let {{child}} own the plan and the assembly — the AI tools are just instruments in {{child}}'s hands. If it gets overwhelming, shrink the scope; a tidy small mashup beats a messy big one.

10. Follow-up Activities

  • Plan (on paper) a bigger mashup {{child}} would like to make next time.
