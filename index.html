Questions parents ask

Good to know.

We don’t live in Kenya. Can my child join?

Yes. Online courses work from anywhere, and live classes run online 1:1 or in small groups, scheduled in your time zone. Prices show in US$, and you can pay by card.

What does my child need?

A computer or tablet with internet for online lessons. AI courses need nothing else. Arduino courses use the Tinkerwith Maker Kit, which you can buy once and reuse in every course, or build free in the Tinkercad simulator first.

How do I pay?

In Kenya, by M-Pesa (Till 3022049; the prompt shows Wakara Technologies Limited, the company behind Tinkerwith). From abroad, by card. Online courses unlock once we’ve checked the payment, usually the same day. Schools can ask for an invoice.

What if it’s not right for my child?

Live courses: not happy after the first session? Full refund. Online courses: not happy within 7 days? Full refund. Just message us.

Is it safe for children?

We never ask for a child’s photo or surname, parents can sign up on their child’s behalf, and AI lessons teach kids to stay safe online. See our privacy policy.

Where do we start?

With the free AI Skills for Kids course (ages 7–9), the free games and activities, or a chat with Anne on WhatsApp to pick the right course.
