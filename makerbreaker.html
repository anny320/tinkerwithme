The golden rules of breaking things

🔌
Unplug first

Never open anything that's plugged in. Pull out the batteries too.

🧑‍🔧
Grown-up nearby

Do teardowns with an adult, on a clear table, with safety glasses on.

🚫
Some things stay shut

No microwaves, TVs/monitors, or anything with big capacitors. Recycle those whole.

🔋
Respect batteries

Never bend, poke or heat a lithium battery. Keep it sealed and recycle it.

Join a MakerBreaker lab

Once a quarter we run a hands-on MakerBreaker session — exactly how we started back in 2015. Kids bring in old, dead electronics and take them apart to see how they work and harvest the useful bits. It's messy, it's curious, and everyone leaves with a bag of parts and a project idea.

  • Bring your own broken electronics (small, safe gadgets — no microwaves or TVs)
  • Learn to open, identify and harvest components safely
  • Swap parts with other makers and plan a build
  • Tools and step-by-step guidance provided — we run every teardown safely

Ages 8+. Younger makers welcome with a parent. Please bring safety glasses if you have a pair (even sunglasses help). Venue in Nairobi — confirmed by email, along with our safety guide, once you book.
