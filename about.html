About us

Behind the brand.

We’re not just about teaching — we’re about inspiring.

Our mission

Turn curiosity into creation.

Founded by educators and tech enthusiasts, TinkerWith_ curates practical tools, step-by-step guides and interactive lessons that make learning electronics, robotics and AI accessible to everyone — from beginners who have never touched a circuit board to teens ready to code their first robot.

Our courses are built for children aged 6 and up, whatever their starting point. Learning should be a fun and interactive experience, not a lecture.

We are not just teaching skills. We are nurturing the next generation of thinkers, makers and doers.
What we teach

Three strands, one workbench.

They overlap constantly — a robot needs circuits, and an AI needs somewhere to live.

Robotics

Robots aren’t just science fiction — they’re part of daily life. We teach kids and teens to design, build and program robots from scratch.

What you’ll do

  • Program basic movement with block-based coding
  • Build line-following bots and obstacle avoiders
  • Take on a robotic arm or an autonomous vehicle

Technology

Behind every great invention is a foundation in circuits, sensors and microcontrollers. This is the gateway to those building blocks.

What you’ll do

  • Resistors, capacitors, LEDs and breadboards
  • Arduino, Raspberry Pi and Micro:bit
  • Sensors and actuators — make things react

AI

Artificial intelligence isn’t just for engineers. It’s for dreamers, thinkers and creators — and it can be understood without writing a line of code.

What you’ll do

  • What makes a machine “smart”, and how it learns
  • Train a model with Teachable Machine
  • Build something real — a sorting bin, a chatbot
