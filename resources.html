Teach with what you have

Run every Tinkerwith project with everyday technology and cheap local parts. One small core buy — a microcontroller — and everything else you can salvage, source locally, or skip with the free simulator.

← Back to the Workbench

🧠

The one thing to buy: a microcontroller

It’s the “brain” every Arduino project shares — an Arduino Uno or Nano, or a low-cost clone for a few hundred shillings, reused across all nine projects. Can’t buy one yet? Build and test the whole circuit free in the Tinkercad simulator first. Everything else on this page, you can tinker with what you have.

The salvage shelf

Start a classroom box. These everyday things hide the parts most projects need — ask families and repair shops to save them.

What each project needs

The Optional add-on is a cheap part to buy only if you can’t salvage it. Everything under You already have comes from the salvage shelf.

🔧 Arduino · 9 projects

🏠 Smart Home Builder

HaveCardboard house, salvaged LEDs, a card buzzer, an LDR from a remote, bottle-cap buttons.

Add-onA PIR motion sensor, or a small servo for a door.

🚗 Self-Driving Car

HaveMotors & wheels from a toy car, a cardboard or bottle chassis, AA holder or power bank, a bottle-cap caster.

Add-onA motor driver + an ultrasonic or IR sensor.

🏙️ Smart City

HaveCardboard blocks, salvaged LEDs for traffic lights and street lamps, an LDR, a buzzer.

Add-onNone needed.

🦾 Robot Arm & Claw

HaveIce-cream sticks, cardboard, bottle caps and rubber bands for the arm (or syringes for unplugged hydraulics).

Add-on2–4 hobby servos — the one part worth buying here.

⌚ Wearable Tech Lab

HaveAn old cap or T-shirt, salvaged LEDs, foil tape as a conductive track, a coin or AA battery.

Add-onA tilt or temperature sensor, if you want sensing.

🌱 IoT Garden Monitor

HaveTwo nails + wire for a DIY soil-moisture probe, a bottle planter, salvaged LEDs.

Add-onA temperature / humidity sensor.

🎹 Musical Instrument Builder

HaveA speaker or piezo from old electronics, buttons from a remote, a potentiometer from a radio, a cardboard body.

Add-onNone needed.

🪴 Smart Plant Carer

HaveA DIY nail moisture probe, a bottle reservoir, LED or buzzer alerts from salvage.

Add-onA mini water pump, for auto-watering.

📶 Bluetooth Control Lab

HaveYour phone as the remote, salvaged LEDs and motors.

Add-onA Bluetooth module (HC-05 / HC-06).

🏙️🤖 Combo

🏙️ Smart City 2.0 — AI-Powered

HaveThe Smart City salvage above, plus a phone or laptop for the AI parts.

Add-onSame as Smart City — the AI runs in a browser.

🤖 AI · 7 projects
No kit at all. The seven AI courses — AI Game Designer, AI Movie Studio, AI Entrepreneur, Deepfake Detective & AI Safety, AI Music Producer, Computer Vision Explorer and AI for Social Good — need only a phone, tablet or computer with a browser, and free tools (including the Tinkerwith AI tutor). Computer Vision Explorer uses the device’s camera. A shared device for a small group is plenty.

A starting point — check it against the curriculum

These are suggestions to spark local sourcing, not a parts list. Always follow a project’s own materials list, and test salvaged parts before class. Want help adapting a project to what your learners can find locally? Message Anne on WhatsApp or head back to the Workbench.
