Our projects start from everyday things — an old phone, a toy, a solar lamp, cheap local parts — applied to problems they recognise. How to teach with Tinkerwith

1. Pick your material

Grab a ready-made curriculum, generate your own, or start today with the 34 free courses — no sign-up needed to try them.

2. Use what you have

Start with everyday tech and cheap local parts; build free in the Tinkercad simulator; AI runs in a browser. An optional Maker Kit is there if you’d rather buy a ready-made set.

3. Teach & celebrate

Run the sessions, learners build something real, and every learner earns a certificate to show off.

Good to know

Materials

Start with everyday tech and cheap local parts, or build free in the Tinkercad simulator; AI needs only a browser. Prefer a ready-made set? The Tinkerwith Maker Kit is an optional convenience, reused across every course.

Curriculum licences

A curriculum is KES 1,500 per teacher. Teaching a whole school or running a club for pay? Ask about a school or multi-teacher licence.

Payment

Pay by M-Pesa (Till 3022049 — the prompt shows Wakara Technologies Limited, the company behind Tinkerwith) or by card from outside Kenya.
