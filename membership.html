What’s the difference between Free, Club and Premium?

They differ by how much live time and cohort you get — the courses come included. Free: the 34 projects, the Builds Wall and certificates. Club: a monthly live group session with a Kenyan trainer, the AI self-paced courses, a new build each month and the maker community. Premium: a small live cohort (max 8 kids, your own trainer, two classes a month), all 17 courses including the robotics lab, parent progress reports and a termly demo day.

How do the live cohorts work? Will there be a spot?

Premium cohorts are deliberately small — max 8 kids with their own trainer — so every child is seen. We have a team of 10 trainers running several cohorts in parallel, grouped by age and level, so there’s a spot and a time that fits. Club’s monthly session is a larger group open to all members.

Do we need to buy a kit?

No. The AI courses run in a browser, and the robotics courses are built to work with everyday parts you already have plus a cheap microcontroller and a free simulator — our resources guide shows how. A Maker Kit is an optional convenience if you’d rather have everything in one box; it’s never required.

What if we change our mind?

You’re covered by a 10-day money-back guarantee — a full refund within 10 days if you haven’t completed a course or joined a live session. Monthly plans aren’t locked in; cancel any time.

Can we invite a friend?

Yes — refer a friend and you both get a free month once they join. Message us on WhatsApp with your friend’s name after they sign up and we’ll add the month to both accounts.

When does everything go live?

The free projects and self-paced AI courses are available now. The live sessions, robotics lab, parent progress reports and demo days are rolling out — new members get them as each cohort opens.

How do I pay?

By card (Visa/Mastercard via Paystack) — your membership switches on straight away and auto-renews, cancel any time. Or by M-Pesa (Till 3022049 — the prompt shows Wakara Technologies Limited), which we activate once we’ve checked your payment, usually the same day.
