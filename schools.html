How it works

1. Tell us about your learners

Ages, group size, the term or dates you have in mind, and what you’d like them to learn. Use the form below or WhatsApp.

2. Get a plan and a quote

Anne sends a proposal with the projects, session timings, kits and a price per learner.

3. We build together

We run the sessions (or hand your teachers the curriculum), learners build real projects, and every learner gets a certificate.

Good to know

Ages and levels

Ages 5 to 16, from first circuits and “what is AI?” to Arduino robots, computer vision and AI projects. We match the projects to your learners.

Kits and equipment

We bring the kits for camps and workshops. For term programmes, schools can buy the Tinkerwith Maker Kit, which is reused across every Arduino course. AI projects use free tools in a browser.

Invoices and payment

We invoice from Wakara Technologies Limited, the company behind Tinkerwith. Pay by bank transfer, M-Pesa (Till 3022049) or card.

Safeguarding and privacy

We follow your school’s safeguarding and photo policies. Learners’ details are only used to run the programme, as set out in our privacy policy.
